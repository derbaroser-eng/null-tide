## What it is

NULL TIDE is a browser god-game with no avatar, no combat, no grid to optimise and
no game-over screen. You are the tide itself: you inhale (recede) and exhale (flood)
over a tiny procedural city, and the city remembers everything you left behind —
then rewrites its own culture around it.

Every run ends the same way. The Deep Bell rings, the tide leaves forever, and the
city writes your **eulogy**: a four-line poem and a title such as
*THE DROWNER*, *THE HALF-MERCIFUL* or *THE LAMP-LIGHTER*. That is the score.

## Why it is not another jam clone

1. **You play a verb, not a character.** Two buttons and a mouse. That is the whole control scheme.
2. **Memory is the only resource.** Water deposits and steals objects — a shoe, a brass
   bell, a wedding ring, an oil drum. Citizens pick them up and build shrines, taboos
   and festivals around them. Leave a sword twice and the district becomes **Militarist**.
   Leave a teddy bear and it becomes **Tender**. This is a culture-sim, not an economy-sim.
3. **Every run produces a shareable artifact.** The game renders a 1200x630 memory card
   (eulogy + pixel skyline + seed) you can save as a PNG. Players post
   "my city remembered me as MERCIFUL / DROWNER" — that is the viral hook.
4. **No server, no account, no tracking.** The seed recreates the city 1:1, so any
   memory card is reproducible. 100% original code, $0 spent, zero dependencies.

## Features

- 14 story objects with weight / echo / stain, 5 districts with hidden Reverence and Dread meters
- 6 omens (Storm, Gull Plague, Deep Bell Echo, Moon Pull, Stranger's Boat, Oil Slick), each a real trade-off
- 15 authored eulogies + 9 fallbacks — **24 distinct endings**, verified reachable by an automated test
- Procedural city name, skyline and ambient generative audio (WebAudio, mutable)
- Seed entry: dare a friend to beat your eulogy with the same seed
- Reduced-motion and safe-mode toggles; touch and mouse both supported
