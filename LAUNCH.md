## Launch-day post (copy/paste, add the live URL)

Title: **I made a god-game where you play the tide, not the hero. No combat, no
game over — the city writes a 4-line poem about you at the end.**

Body: NULL TIDE is a 12-minute browser game. You inhale (recede) and exhale
(flood) over a tiny procedural city. Objects you leave behind — a shoe, a bell,
a wedding ring — get picked up by citizens who build shrines and taboos around
them. Leave a sword twice and the district turns Militarist; leave a teddy bear
and it turns Tender. Every run ends with a shareable 1200x630 eulogy card
("my city remembered me as THE HALF-MERCIFUL"). 24 endings, seeds, no server,
no tracking, free in the browser. Play: <URL>.
